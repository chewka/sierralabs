    ABOUT THIS SIMULATION

    A tiny autonomous laboratory. Press play and the scientist reads the active workflow, works out which step is ready, walks to the right equipment, does the work, records it, and moves on — with no input from you. Then you can go and cause problems.

    What the numbers are

    Every measurement, curve, chromatogram and percentage in this game is produced by a small toy model written into the simulation. None of it is measured data, and none of it reproduces numeric values from any publication. The shape of each workflow — the order of the steps, what depends on what, and which way the results tend to move — follows standard published laboratory methodology for this kind of work.

    The “Published finding / Simulated outcome” switch in the results panel makes that split explicit: one side shows the direction a finding takes in the methodology being modelled, the other shows the numbers this simulation just invented.

    What it does not do

    • It contains no protocols: no formulations, no concentrations, no strain-construction detail. Steps are described at the level of “prepare the precursor”, which is what you would see watching from the doorway.
    • Experiment D is laboratory and in-vitro research only. Nothing in it is a treatment or evidence of one, and its gel-handling steps are an openly simplified stand-in rather than a reconstructed procedure.
    • Nothing hazardous happens, and no unsafe shortcut is ever rewarded.

    Workflow sources

    The four workflows are modelled on the methodology chapters of a 2023 doctoral dissertation on engineered living material platforms (hydrogel preservation; reusable encapsulated protein production; a three-module microbial consortium; an engineered probiotic biosensor). The simulation reproduces the sequence of work from those chapters, not their data.
